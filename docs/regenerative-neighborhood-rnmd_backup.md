How Regenerative Neighborhoods work
Regenerative Neighborhoods are spaces designed to support safety, growth, connection, and creation. 
Regenerative Neighborhood nurture : 
the journey of you “being your best self”,
connection to other humans being their best selves,
connection to and a deeper understanding of nature,
collaboration to create things that bring value to your self, others, and the planet.
 
Regenerative Neighborhoods provide physical infrastrucure and activities for
individual well-being
collective well-being
connection to nature
purpose-driven creation
 
To build these places, we leverage :
🛠️ hardware
🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒 humanware
🤝 economy (exchangeware)
💻 software (My.Co.Net)
Regenerative Neighborhoods are designed to :
 meet human needs
support positive social connections
connect us to nature
& accelerate collaborations that help the planet.
They are :
Good for the individual
Good for the collective
Good for the planet

What is the RNMD​
The RNMD Regenerative Neighborhood modular design is a scalable framework to build a Regenerative Neighborhood and all its components.

hardware • humanware • economy • software

What is a Regenerative Neighborhood ?
A Regenerative Neighborhood is 

🏡wellness realestate,
💧🍎⚡️🌬️♻️with resillient resource systems,
🧑‍🧑‍🧒‍🧒community lifestyle & amenities.
🌱Regenerative Neighborhoods foster connection & understanding with nature.
💰They are economically sustainable & productive.
🤝Regenerative Neighborhoods are connected in an opt in mutual support network,
🤖And they operate with an LLM interagted application.  

🌍Regenerative Neighborhoods are designed to create positive impact. 

 They aim to create regenerative 

infrastructure,
culture,
economy
technology
– solutions that improve the status quo.

Where :
Our vision is Regenerative Neighborhoods around the world and accesible to every human. 

 Our purpose is for every person on the planet to have their needs met and live in alignemnt with nature.

Therefore, we are using an open source approach  to support the creation of Regenerative Neighborhoods everywhere
