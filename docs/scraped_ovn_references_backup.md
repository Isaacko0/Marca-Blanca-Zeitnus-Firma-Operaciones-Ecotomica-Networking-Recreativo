## 1. Multitude Project — "The sharing, collaborative and participatory economy: what are we talking about?" (2016-05-29)

**URL**: https://multitudeproject.blogspot.com/2016/05/the-sharing-collaborative-and.html  
**Autor**: TiberiusB (Tiberius Brastaviceanu, co-fundador Sensorica)  
**Nota**: **Fuente original** de la Sección 2 del modelo económico OVN (economía compartida/colaborativa/participativa)

### Contenido Clave (Extraído)

> **Distinciones semánticas basadas en experiencia Sensorica OVN:**

**Economía Compartida (Sharing Economy)**:
- Énfasis en recursos/activos "compartidos" — acceso **no transaccional**, mutualizados, redistribuidos, circulados recíprocamente sin ánimo de lucro
- Sharing = **gift** (unidireccional) o **barter** (bidireccional)
- Transferencias de ownership o custodianship según property regime
- Recursos en commons/nondominium/pool of shareables → meant to be shared
- **Crítica**: término usado abusivamente para selling/renting (Airbnb/Uber) → Harvard review: "misnomer, reemplazar por access economy"
- Uso del activo irrelevante si se preserva integridad y reglas acceso

**Economía Colaborativa (Collaborative Economy)**:
- Énfasis en **allocation** de tiempo/recursos para **common output**
- Proceso minimiza intercambios mediados por monedas monetarias
- Forma pura: **nadie paga a nadie**, nadie monopoliza producción
- Ejemplos: Linux, Wikipedia, open source HW/SW
- Empresas tradicionales = intercambio skills/time por money → NO collaborative economy
- Reglas para leverage output → licencias (no restriction → non-commercial)
- Extensible a contributions: financial (crowdfunding), material (3D printer sharing)
- Contribuciones tangibles = project/venture specific (vs sharing economy donde uso es personal)
- No transfer ownership, no rental → shared within group for common use
- Redistribución beneficios: reconocimiento público O equity (promesa beneficios futuros → **Benefit redistribution algorithm**)

**Economía Participativa (Participatory Economy)**:
- Énfasis en **process** mediante el cual se crea colaborativamente
- Adherir/acceder al proceso, participar en él
- Proceso **abierto**, barreras entrada muy bajas/nulas (permissionless, ej. Bitcoin)
- Sensorica = permissionless
- Coops tradicionales = collaborative production PERO acceso condicionado (hiring/invitation)
- Enspiral/las Indias = coops, NO redes abiertas/permissionless
- Participatory economy = relaciones que otorgan acceso a procesos productivos a cualquiera que pueda deliver, no other questions asked

**Ambos términos (collaborative + participatory) aplican a OVN** porque:
- Allocation tiempo/recursos = crowdsourcing
- Assets preferidos en commons/nondominium/shareable pools
- Acceso red/ventures = permissionless

## 2. Multitude Project — "Networks of networks" (2022-03-01)

**URL**: https://multitudeproject.blogspot.com/2022/03/networks-of-networks.html  
**Autor**: Tibi (Tiberius Brastaviceanu)  
**Nota**: Borrador inicial, copiado de email — expande Sección 18.2 (Network of Networks level)

### Contenido Clave

> **Living systems theory** at the core of p2p. Life requires flow. Organized flows require support and structure.

**Open networks = fractal**:
- Made of smaller open networks, coalesce into larger open networks
- "Open networks 'try to discover' new ways to connect among themselves. It's in their nature"
- Blockchain space: L2s, cross-chain connections = open networks connecting

**Interconnectivity without intermediaries**:
- "We should not need intermediaries to be able to connect open networks together"
- Open networks should plug into each other directly via shared protocol/attachment sites
- Biology analogy: molecules/proteins interact based on internal structure, affinities, biochemical rules
- Current platform paradigm (automate.io, IFTTT) = wrong approach (connection as service, middlemen)
- **Design paradigm shift needed**: interconnectivity as design priority
- Discord bots/webhooks = direct pipes (better model)

**Linkages allow new flows**:
- Gitcoin crowdfunding: increases metabolism + stimulates networks to build connections
- "Neurons that fire together network together" — networks connect if rewarded together
- Gitcoin grants bundling (collections) = shared funding as stimulus to grow connections
- Sensorica grant + TaoDao grant = portion of funds to build internal structures for interconnection

## 3. P2P Foundation Wiki — "Peer Production" (Comprehensive)

**URL**: https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/Peer_Production  
**Contenido**: 50KB+ article — definiciones, características, building blocks, governance, conditions

### Hallazgos Clave Adicionales (vs backup OVN)

#### Four Constituent Building Blocks (Christian):
1. **Voluntary cooperation among peers** — goal-driven, stigmergic coordination (hints about what to do, others voluntarily follow)
2. **Peer-based** — individuals autonomous, decentralized conception/execution
3. **Commons-based** — driven by general interest, open access licenses, technically available
4. **Reproducibility and Derivativeness** — forkability (license allows derivative work)

#### Ten Social Patterns of Peer Production (from p2pValue project):
*[Truncated in extract — need to read full article]*

#### Governance:
- Participatory governance
- Consensus decision-making
- No distinction between workers and managers (pure peer production)

#### Conditions for Success (Yochai Benkler):
- Low transaction costs
- Modularity of tasks
- Low cost of integration
- Diverse motivations

#### Is Peer Production Beyond Capitalism?:
- Capitalist motivations for supporting open source: commodification of complements, talent acquisition, standards setting, marketing
- Hybrid models: produce free goods but control official distribution (MySQL, Canonical/Ubuntu, Mozilla pre-2005)

#### Three Criteria for Peer Production (Michel Bauwens - Circulation of the Common):
1. **Open input**: contributors free to contribute, access to free/open raw material
2. **Process**: participatory governance
3. **Output**: put in commons for iterative creation of new open input layers

#### Produsage (Axel Bruns):
- Consumer/producer distinction faded → "produser"
- Collaborative, ad hoc engagement with content
- Production term no longer accurate for user-led content creation

## 4. Harvard Business Review — "The Sharing Economy Isn't About Sharing at All" (2015-01-28)

**URL**: https://hbr.org/2015/01/the-sharing-economy-isnt-about-sharing-at-all  
**Autores**: Giana M. Eckhardt & Fleura Bardhi  
**Nota**: Paywalled — solo thesis accesible

### Thesis Principal (del abstract accesible):
> "The sharing economy isn't really a 'sharing' economy at all; it's an **access economy**."
- Disrupted hotels/automotives via convenient, cost-efficient access without ownership burdens
- Term "sharing economy" = marketing scheme by Airbnb/Uber to obfuscate rent-extraction model
- Confirmed by OVN model's distinction: sharing economy = access economy (transactional), NOT sharing economy (non-transactional, reciprocal)
