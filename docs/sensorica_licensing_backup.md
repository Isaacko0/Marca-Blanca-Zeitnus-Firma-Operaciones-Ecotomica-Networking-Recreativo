### Introducción
> "There is no one-fit-all type solution for hardware licensing. It depends on the nature of the artifact, as well as on the context in which the artifact is brought into existence and is distributed. Every situation defines a specific **game** (game theory). We need to understand what possible games we can play, to choose our games and to learn how to play them well."

**License = degree of control** over creations → influences adoption, dissemination, development, end of life. Control not always needed, sometimes detrimental; sometimes necessary for sustainability/protection.

### 6 Basic Assumptions
1. **Sensorica brand protected** — logo copyrighted, only affiliates in sanctioned operations, moderated by reputation system + positive incentives OVN. Brand = part of **commons**.
2. **Open source hardware/software** — modular, easily updated, NOT programmed for obsolescence, NOT made difficult to modify. Cannot be copied by traditional business (different philosophy). Ver Design Philosophy.
3. **Ecosystem of interoperable hardware** — interdependence makes copying single artifact harder. Brand, quality, ethical/moral dimension play in favor.
4. **Community of developers/designers/users** around open hardware — closed hardware cannot do this.
5. **Trans-organizational/trans-national collaboration** on open source HW/SW.
6. Add others...

## Basic Assumptions (Detailed)

> Read Tibi's post: **How to play the open game in the present and future economy**

**Repeated Game Theory** assumption:
- Tactic works when hardware solution leads to **repeated and long-term game** between OVN affiliates (researchers, designers, manufacturers, marketers, distributors...) and users
- Arises for: material artifacts with rapid evolution (electronic devices), high reproduction costs, special knowledge for marketing/servicing, part of complex ecosystem
- High-tech products fall in this category
- High dependency between designers ↔ fabricators/manufacturers/distributors
- Once manufacturing/distribution network developed → economic situation improves (brand, distribution, economy of scale)
- Sensorica developing distributed manufacturing infrastructure → **Nondominium** (GitHub: Sensorica/nondominium)

## Discussion

### Low-cost, one-time, wide adoption hardware solutions
**Game theory analysis**: One-time game (not repeated) → fabricator doesn't depend on designer for repeated innovation.

**Case: Hockey stick with force sensor** (Francois question)
- Nike could copy design, mass produce in China, steal rewards via brand/distribution
- **Tibi's answer**: Nike sells sports equipment, not sensors. Would partner with Sensorica for force sensing device. Problem: if Sensorica can't produce at Nike's cost dictation → Nike goes to Chinese copy. Sensorica needs partner at Nike's cost.
- **Key insight**: Mass market one-time product = weak link → Nike can jump over Sensorica.

### Multiple Licenses
> **[Tibi]**: "If there is no one-fit-all solution we can use different licenses for different hardware solutions, based on the type of game each type of artifact and economic context entails."

### General Discussion — Bayle Shanks
> "The idea that the value network license is noncommercial use only and the sensor license allows commercial use seems backwards to me. What if a company wants to use the value network? We won't sell it so why not give it. And what if a company wants to use sensor designs? This is something we'd prefer to sell, so why give it away?"

**Bayle's proposed license**: **Slowly_Opening_License** (p2pfoundation.net) — not yet drafted, needs better name. Patents for defensive use only.

### [Tibi] On Open Innovation
> "In the open innovation philosophy knowledge is considered as being abundant. Moreover, we believe that sharing/spreading knowledge can bring more than keeping it and exploiting it alone or at small scale. Furthermore, knowledge is NOT seen as property. There is no jealous attachment to one's ideas. The FOCUS is on the effects of ideas AND on the process through which wealth is extracted from applied ideas and knowledge. We believe that there are new such processes available. Control of ideas of knowledge is NOT necessary, even more, its detrimental! Rewarding knowledge or ideas directly becomes incompatible with the game. This is why I like to say that we are moving, either we like it or not, towards a **know how economy rather than a knowledge economy**, i.e. people will get paid for what they do, not for their ideas, which is not to say that ideas will become unimportant, they will not be considered as products anymore, because not only they are becoming abundant, but also because it becomes more 'profitable' when ideas are shared than when they are kept. These processes are new and they are related to large scale effects made possible by the Internet. A new game is now possible because of the Internet and digital technology, the **open innovation game**. In my opinion, this is what we need to master. Why is it counter-intuitive? Because we've been conditioned NOT to share and to compete. This game is about sharing and collaboration. Moreover, this game introduces large scale effects, and humans are used to think at small scale, small group dynamics..."

## Hallazgos Clave para Zeitnus

### 1. Game Theory as Licensing Framework
- **Repeated game** (high-tech, rapid evolution, complex ecosystem) → open licenses work
- **One-time game** (low-cost, trendy, mass market) → need protection (CERN OHL, patents)
- **No one-fit-all** → multiple licenses per artifact type/game

### 2. OVN Model = CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 | Sensing Tech = CC BY-SA 3.0 / CERN OHL / OVN License (2025)
- **Commercial use allowed** for sensing tech (explicit)
- **Non-commercial** for OVN model (framework)

### 3. Know-How Economy vs Knowledge Economy
- **Focus on effects of ideas + process of wealth extraction** — NOT control of ideas
- Knowledge = abundant, NOT property
- Sharing ideas more profitable than keeping
- **Internet enables new game: open innovation game**

### 4. Nondominium = Distributed Manufacturing Infrastructure
- GitHub: Sensorica/nondominium (branch be7d8e23...)
- Enables economy of scale for Sensorica affiliates

### 5. Slowly Opening License (Bayle Shanks) — Alternative Model
- Progressive opening over time
- Patents defensive only
- p2pfoundation.net/Slowly_Opening_License
