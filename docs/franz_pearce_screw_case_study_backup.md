## Abstract

> **Challenge**: Enable free and open-source hardware (FOSH) to catch up with FOSS by identifying appropriate business models.
> 
> **New FOSH business model**: **Specialty components for fabricators** — case study of open-source screw manufacturer business.
> 
> **Context**: Distributed Recycling and Additive Manufacturing (DRAM) space.
> 
> **Analysis**: Component payback time under various scenarios, sales for enticing income, business expansion threshold.
> 
> **Results**: **>1,000 small businesses** could follow **Do-It-Together (DIT)** approach sharing FOSH designs while manufacturing and profiting locally to serve burgeoning DRAM market.
> 
> **JEL Codes**: L17, O36

## Marco Teórico: Design Global, Manufacture Local (DGML)

- **DGML** = local fabrication using global information exchange (Kostakis et al., 2018)
- Form of **commons-based peer production (CBPP)** (Bauwens 2005; Kostakis et al. 2011; Ostrom 1990)
- Intellectual resources shared for common benefit (Ostrom 1990)
- Replicates FOSS success (Bonaccorsi & Rossi 2003; Lakhani & Von Hippel 2003)
- Enabled by: 3D printers, CNC mills for PCBs, physical products maturation (Gwamuri et al. 2014)
- **Maker Movement** → democratization of industrialization (Dougherty 2012)
- DIY distributed production → personal fabrication, desktop manufacturing
- Networks of makerspaces/fab labs/hacker spaces surged (Barton et al. 2016)
- Prosumption at all levels — local materials, local innovation/entrepreneurship

## Case Study: Open-Source Industrial-Scale CNC Screw Manufacturing

### Contexto DRAM
- **Distributed Recycling and Additive Manufacturing (DRAM)**: recycle thermopolymers → 3D printer filament
- **Recyclebot** (desktop) + **industrial-scale** systems
- **Compression screws** = critical component for both recyclebot and industrial extruders
- **Thermopolymers demonstrated in DRAM**: PLA, ABS, PET, HIPS, PP, HDPE, LDPE, etc.

### Business Model: Specialty Component Manufacturer
- **Open-source screw manufacturer** → sells specialty screws to other fabricators/prosumers in DRAM
- **Do-It-Together (DIT)** approach: share FOSH designs, manufacture locally, profit locally
- **Not DIY** — DIT = collaboration across distributed network

### Economic Analysis

#### Payback Time Calculation
- Open-source industrial CNC screw manufacturing machine
- Scenarios: varying operating costs
- **Break-even**: number of recyclebot/desktop screws to manufacture

#### Sales for $100K Profit/Year (before overhead)
- Pricing scenarios quantified
- Quantities sold at different price points

#### Business Expansion Threshold
- Point at which expansion necessary determined

#### Distributed DIT Businesses Estimate
- **>1,000 small businesses** could follow DIT approach
- Share FOSH designs, manufacture locally, profit locally
- Serve burgeoning DRAM market

## Ventajas del Modelo DIT (vs DIY)

| Aspecto | DIY (Do-It-Yourself) | DIT (Do-It-Together) |
|---------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Design** | Individual | Shared FOSH designs (global) |
| **Manufacturing** | Local, isolated | Local, networked |
| **Innovation** | Individual | Collaborative, rapid |
| **Risk** | High (alone) | Shared (community feedback) |
| **Market Access** | Individual | Network channels |
| **Scale** | Limited | >1000 businesses potential |

## Hallazgos Clave para Zeitnus

### 1. DIT = **Operationalización de CBPP/DGML** en Manufacturing
- No solo "compartir diseños" — **manufacturing colaborativo distribuido**
- Specialty components = **high-value, low-volume** (scientific tools: 87% savings vs proprietary)
- **Payback time calculable** → viable business model metrics

### 2. DRAM Space = **Circular Economy + FOSH** Integration
- Recycle thermopolymers → filament → 3D printing
- Compression screws = **critical shared component** (recyclebot + industrial)
- **>1000 small businesses** = scalable distributed manufacturing network

### 3. FOSH Business Models Validados Empíricamente
- Kit sales, Specialty components, Calibration, Education, Ecosystem services, Trust frameworks
- **Community reduces costs & time-to-market** via 5 mechanisms (Li & Seering 2019)
- **Lean startup** possible: extremely low overhead

### 4. Specialty Components = **Zeitnus CaaS Service Template** Candidate
- "Vitamins" (non-digitally fabricated components) → **DIY Kit template**
- Hard-to-find parts → **Specialty Component template**
- Calibration/validation → **Service template**
- Education → **Education template**

### 5. DIT > DIY para Zeitnus
- **DIY** = individual, isolated
- **DIT** = networked, collaborative, shared designs + local manufacturing
- **Zeitnus handshake generativo** = DIT protocol at organism level
