## Definición Core

> **Labonline** = laboratorio físico conteniendo instrumentos que pueden:
> - Enviar datos por Internet en **tiempo real**
> - Proporcionar **acceso remoto en tiempo real** para control de instrumentos
> - Permitir a individuos (equipo de expertos con diferentes skills) de **diferentes partes del mundo** compartir experiencias en tiempo real

> **Open Labonline Network** = red de open physical labs, basada en infraestructura para almacenamiento e intercambio de datos (incluso tiempo real)

> **Idea**: Construir open labonline network trayendo juntos **diferentes open lab spaces, academic labs y otros, via the web**.

## Expanding via Government Funding

> **Value proposition for government**: Every startup needs space/tools for prototyping/PoC. Instead of funding lab per startup (high risk, high cost), fund **few hackerspaces/fab labs** as **shared resource centers**.

**Benefits**:
- Diminishes initial load on government assistance
- **Networking centers** — startups meet, exchange ideas, collaborate
- Sensorica affiliates → managers/technicians for open labs
- Open lab members → become Sensorica members (bidirectional flow)

---

## Expanding by Affiliation (Affiliation Model)

> **Analogy**: Public transportation vs Taxi
> - Small companies exchange services at market prices (taxi) → too expensive
> - **Open labs = public transportation** — shared resource, public funds, marginal cost exchange
> - Multi-value system: value exchanged outside monetary system
> - Incentivizes small orgs/individuals to populate open labonline network

**Key Insight**: "Environment in which value could be exchanged outside of the monetary system, in a multi-value system."
